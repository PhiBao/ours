Take-home guide

Ours

Two people who share money each answer six open questions alone, then open the same page. Everything below is a pointer into the real code, so you can find any behaviour you see on screen.

The one idea worth remembering

Nobody goes first

Every product in shared finances answers an information problem but asks you to solve a trust problem first: someone has to link accounts or open up, and going first feels like conceding, so it never happens. Both people argue from partial pictures forever.

Ours inverts that. The reveal does not exist until both people are sealed in, and neither ever sees the other's words.

The three files that matter

If you only read three things

FileWhy
src/lib/compose.tsEvery sentence on the reveal. It has no imports and no I/O — numbers go in, English comes out. All the product's voice is in this one file.
src/lib/questions.tsThe measurements, each phrased as the exact claim it licenses, with the calibration evidence in the comments.
src/lib/db.tsThe privacy claim lives in storeRevealAndShred: it writes the page and clears both answer sets in one transaction.

The practice worth reusing: before shipping a product that says something sensitive about someone, write the part that makes the promise structurally true — then check it from outside. Here that check is pnpm e2e, which drives the real app over HTTP and then reads the database directly to confirm both people's answers are gone. "We don't store it" is a claim; raw_answers = NULL is a fact.

Prove it yourself

Three commands

  • pnpm seed — the pipeline on a real pair, then three controls that must come back silent. If the aligned pair ever produces a finding, the thresholds have drifted and the product is making claims it should not.
  • pnpm e2e — the full loop over HTTP, ending in a direct read of the database.
  • pnpm demo — the demonstration control: change one answer, watch a finding disappear.

The one thing to be careful with

Thresholds are measured, not guessed

0.65 for "strong enough to show", and a movement also needs real confidence in the answer. Those numbers came from running four written pairs, not from taste. They are recorded in questions.ts with the values they were set from.

If you change a question, re-run pnpm seed and read the controls before believing it.
